CREWMATE

A social deduction game — find the impostor among your crew.

How to play
  • One player hosts on a computer and shares the 4-digit room code.
  • Everyone else opens this same page on their phone, taps Join a game and enters the code.
  • Crewmates must finish all tasks or vote out the impostors. Impostors kill the crew.
  • Phones get automatic touch controls: drag on the left to move, tap the buttons on the right to act.
  • Desktop: WASD / arrows to move, E to use, Q to kill, R to report, M for map.
